Shilp Marathon 2024
700×440px
Environmental Action · 2024

How 40 students changed a marathon's footprint

At Shilp Marathon 2024, ARC Foundation's 40+ volunteers educated 20,000 participants, deployed a Reverse Vending Machine converting plastic to recyclable flakes, and ensured the material went back to underprivileged students as school supplies.

Environmental Action · Ongoing

8 acres brought back to life

The villages of Pratapgarh and Ramnagar now have functioning waste management systems. The bigger story: 8 acres of erosion-damaged earth restored using repurposed foundry sand — unusable land made productive again, through ingenuity and community action.

Social Upliftment · Gandhinagar

A flour mill that changed what was possible

The Adhyashakti Flour Mill is proof: women in rural India, given the right infrastructure and support, can build enterprises that serve their communities. Developed with DRDA and MGNF — designed to be replicated.
