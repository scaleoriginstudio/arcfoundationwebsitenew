IAF Campus Greening

A plantation drive at the Indian Air Force headquarters in Gandhinagar — integrating environmental sustainability into an institutional wellness program.

IAFInstitutional Partner
Mar–Apr2023
GreenerCampus Coverage

Overview

ARC Foundation executed a plantation drive at the Indian Air Force headquarters in Gandhinagar during the Yoga and Meditation Workshop held from 22 March to 21 April 2023.

The initiative was designed to integrate environmental sustainability with institutional well-being programs, contributing to increased green cover while promoting collective responsibility toward the environment.

Project Execution

ARC Foundation led the on-ground implementation of the plantation activity, ensuring structured participation and effective execution within a high-discipline institutional setting. The drive focused on:

  • Organised plantation within the IAF campus
  • Active involvement of participants in sustainability efforts
  • Alignment with broader wellness and environmental objectives

Environmental Impact

The plantation drive contributed to strengthening ecological awareness while delivering measurable environmental benefits within the institutional premises. Key outcomes include:

  • Increase in green cover within the campus
  • Promotion of environmentally responsible practices
  • Encouragement of participatory sustainability efforts

Institutional Engagement

Conducted within the Indian Air Force ecosystem, the initiative reflects ARC Foundation's ability to collaborate in structured environments and deliver impact-driven sustainability interventions.

Impact Highlights

  • Plantation conducted within a high-discipline institutional environment
  • Contribution to increased green cover
  • Engagement of participants in sustainability-focused action
  • Alignment of environmental responsibility with wellness initiatives

Project Outcome

This initiative demonstrates how targeted environmental actions can be effectively integrated into institutional programs, creating a balance between personal well-being and ecological responsibility.
