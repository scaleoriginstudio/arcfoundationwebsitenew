ARC Leagues

ARC Leagues is our flagship competitive debate platform — structured, school-based, developing critical thinking and civic reasoning at scale across Gujarat.

  • Structured competitive format across schools
  • Trained judges and professional debate structure
  • Annual inter-school championship
  • Partnered with Indian Air Force and Army Public Schools

Saturday Spars

Informal weekly debate practice. Consistent, peer-driven, open to all levels. Quietly builds the most communicators.

Speak Easy

A safe, low-stakes space for first-time speakers — designed for students who want to find their voice before they compete.

Mock Parliament

ARC Walks & Land Reclamation

What started as community clean-up walks became a systematic village-level waste management operation — and then something unexpected: 8 acres of degraded land brought back to life using repurposed foundry sand.

  • Village adoption model in Pratapgarh and Ramnagar
  • Waste segregation, collection, and recycling systems
  • 8 acres of erosion-damaged land reclaimed and restored
  • Community-led maintenance after ARC Foundation's exit

Adhyashakti Mill

Adhyashakti Flour Mill

The Adhyashakti Flour Mill is proof of concept: women in rural Gandhinagar, given the right infrastructure and institutional support, built a sustainable enterprise that serves their community.

  • Developed with DRDA Gandhinagar and MGNF
  • Women-led from operations to financial management
  • Established 2022, serving surrounding rural community
  • Replicable model for rural enterprise development
