Walk with a Purpose — ARC Walks & Land Reclamation

What started as community clean-up walks became a systematic waste management operation — and then something unexpected: 8 acres of degraded land brought back to life.

8 acLand Reclaimed
2Villages Adopted
2021Initiated

Overview

ARC Foundation has implemented a comprehensive waste management and land reclamation initiative that integrates community participation with long-term environmental systems.

The project combines clean-up drives, village-level waste infrastructure, and innovative land restoration techniques to address waste management challenges while improving land usability and ecological conditions.

Community Clean-Up Drives (ARC Walks)

The initiative began with "Walk with a Purpose — ARC Walks," a series of community-led clean-up drives designed to mobilise citizens and youth groups. Participants actively engaged in:

  • Collection of plastic and mixed waste from public spaces
  • Promotion of responsible disposal practices
  • Awareness-building around cleanliness and sustainability

These drives played a critical role in improving local sanitation conditions while fostering a culture of shared civic responsibility.

Village Waste Management Systems

Building on community engagement, ARC Foundation adopted the villages of Pratapgarh and Ramnagar to implement structured waste management systems. The intervention included:

  • Regular waste collection mechanisms
  • Segregation at source (plastic, organic, recyclable waste)
  • Introduction of recycling processes

This system-based approach ensures long-term improvements in waste handling, sanitation, and environmental health at the village level.

Land Reclamation Through Waste Innovation

The project extends beyond waste management into land restoration, using innovative techniques to repurpose waste materials such as foundry sand. This method was applied in erosion-prone areas to:

  • Fill degraded land
  • Improve soil stability
  • Restore land usability

As a result, approximately 8 acres of land have been successfully reclaimed — reducing environmental degradation while creating functional land resources.

Environmental Impact

  • Community-driven clean-up operations across multiple locations
  • Active participation from youth groups and volunteers
  • Adoption of 2 villages for structured waste management
  • Implementation of end-to-end waste collection and recycling systems
  • 8 acres of land restored through waste-based reclamation
  • Reduction in soil erosion and improved land usability

Project Outcome

This initiative demonstrates a scalable model for integrated waste management in rural and semi-urban areas, combining grassroots participation with technical solutions. By linking waste management, community engagement, and land restoration, ARC Foundation has created a replicable framework for sustainable environmental development.
