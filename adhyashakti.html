Adhyashakti Flour Mill

A women-led microenterprise in rural Gandhinagar — proof that the right infrastructure and institutional support can build a sustainable livelihood from the ground up.

2022Established
DRDA& MGNF Partnership
WomenLed Enterprise
RuralGandhinagar

Overview

ARC Foundation supported the establishment of a women-led microenterprise — Adhyashakti Flour Mill in Gandhinagar — creating a sustainable model for rural livelihood generation and community development.

The initiative focuses on empowering local women with income-generating opportunities while addressing an essential community need for accessible flour processing infrastructure.

Project Implementation

The flour mill was developed in collaboration with the District Rural Development Agency (DRDA) and the Mahatma Gandhi National Fellowship (MGNF), ensuring strong institutional support and effective on-ground execution. The project was inaugurated by Kuldeep Arya, highlighting its significance within the district's development framework.

Livelihood & Economic Impact

The initiative enables women to actively participate in the local economy by managing and operating the enterprise. Key outcomes include:

  • Creation of sustainable income streams for rural women
  • Strengthening of financial independence and self-reliance
  • Development of entrepreneurial capabilities at the grassroots level

Community Value Creation

Beyond livelihood generation, the flour mill addresses a critical gap in local infrastructure by providing accessible and reliable flour processing services within the community. This reduces dependency on external services and improves convenience for local households.

Impact Highlights

  • Sustainable livelihood opportunities for rural women
  • Strengthening of the local rural economy
  • Community-level infrastructure development
  • Effective multi-stakeholder collaboration

Project Outcome

The Adhyashakti Flour Mill serves as a replicable model for women-led microenterprises in rural India — demonstrating how targeted interventions can drive both economic empowerment and community development.
