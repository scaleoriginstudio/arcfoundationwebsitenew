Our Story

From a question
to a movement

ARC Foundation began with a deceptively simple question: why do so many people care about India's problems but so few feel equipped to act?

We started in Gujarat, working with students in debate halls and then in fields — planting trees, cleaning villages, building systems. What we discovered is that action breeds confidence, and confidence breeds more action.

Today, ARC Foundation runs programs spanning competitive debate leagues, large-scale environmental interventions, and grassroots livelihood initiatives. We've partnered with institutions like the Indian Air Force, Army Public Schools, and the Mahatma Gandhi National Fellowship.

Our work has engaged over 20,000 citizens, planted 1,000 trees, reclaimed 8 acres of degraded land, and helped establish women-led enterprises that sustain families.

"Citizenship isn't a birthright you receive. It's a practice you choose, every day."

What Drives Us

Our Values

01

Action Over Intention

Change happens in the field, not in boardrooms. Every program produces real, measurable outcomes on the ground.

02

Youth at the Centre

Young people aren't the leaders of tomorrow — they're the leaders of today. We design everything with their agency in mind.

03

Community First

Sustainable change requires community ownership. We build relationships, trust, and systems that outlast our presence.

04

Accountability

We hold ourselves to the same standards we ask of our citizens. Every initiative is tracked and reported transparently.

05

Inclusion

Responsibility knows no gender, class, or background. Our programs are designed to reach those most often left behind.

06

Scale with Soul

We want to grow — but never at the cost of depth. Every new program carries the same quality of care as our very first.
