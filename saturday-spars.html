Overview

Saturday Spars is a recurring debate practice platform by ARC Foundation designed to build consistency in public speaking, critical thinking, and structured argumentation among students.

The initiative provides a regular space for participants to engage in debates, ensuring continuous learning and improvement through practice.

Program Structure

Saturday Spars operates as a weekly engagement platform, where students participate in:

  • Structured debate sessions on diverse topics
  • Argument construction and rebuttal exercises
  • Moderated discussions and peer feedback

The consistent format allows participants to refine their skills over time through repetition and active participation.

Skill Development Focus

Through regular sessions, participants develop:

  • Strong communication and public speaking abilities
  • Improved critical thinking and reasoning skills
  • Confidence in articulating ideas clearly and effectively
  • Adaptability in responding to different perspectives

Peer Learning Environment

The platform fosters a collaborative learning environment, where students learn not only through participation but also by observing and engaging with peers. This encourages:

  • Knowledge exchange
  • Constructive feedback
  • Continuous improvement

Impact Highlights

  • Weekly student engagement through structured sessions
  • Consistent skill-building through practice
  • Development of a peer-driven learning environment

Project Outcome

Saturday Spars creates a foundation for long-term development in debate, communication, and analytical thinking — preparing students for competitive platforms and real-world discourse.
