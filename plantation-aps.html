Tree Plantation Drive at Army Public School, Ahmedabad

1,000 saplings. One school campus. A student-led model for hands-on environmental education and long-term conservation.

Overview

ARC Foundation collaborated with Army Public School Ahmedabad Cantt to execute a large-scale tree plantation drive, focused on increasing green cover and promoting environmental responsibility among students.

The initiative combined on-ground plantation activity with student engagement, creating a practical learning experience around sustainability and conservation.

Project Execution

The plantation drive was implemented across the school campus with active participation from students, volunteers, and institutional stakeholders. Key components included:

  • Plantation of 1,000 saplings within the campus
  • Student-led participation in planting and nurturing activities
  • Structured coordination to ensure effective coverage and execution

Student Engagement & Awareness

The initiative placed strong emphasis on hands-on environmental education, enabling students to directly contribute to sustainability efforts. Participants were encouraged to:

  • Understand the importance of tree plantation and green cover
  • Take ownership of saplings through continued care
  • Adopt environmentally responsible practices in daily life

Sustainability Approach

The project incorporated the "Each One Plant One" model to ensure long-term impact, encouraging every participant to take responsibility for at least one sapling. The initiative also aligned with broader environmental efforts, including the Save Soil movement, reinforcing the importance of ecological conservation.

Environmental Impact

The plantation drive delivered measurable outcomes at both environmental and educational levels:

  • 1,000 saplings planted across the campus
  • Increased green cover within an institutional environment
  • Active student participation in sustainability initiatives
  • Long-term commitment to plant care and maintenance

Project Outcome

This initiative demonstrates ARC Foundation's ability to execute high-impact plantation drives within institutional ecosystems, combining environmental action with youth engagement and awareness-building. It serves as a scalable model for integrating education and sustainability through practical participation.
