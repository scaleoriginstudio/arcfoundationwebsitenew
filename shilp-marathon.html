Overview

ARC Foundation partnered with the Shilp Marathon for the second consecutive year as the official Recycling Partner, implementing a structured waste management and sustainability initiative at a large public event.

The project focused on waste segregation, plastic recycling, volunteer mobilisation, and environmental awareness — demonstrating how high-footfall events can be converted into platforms for measurable ecological impact.

Waste Management & Recycling Impact

The initiative delivered tangible outcomes through on-ground execution and efficient waste handling systems:

  • 12,000+ plastic bottles collected
  • 80 kg of plastic waste diverted from landfills
  • Recycled material repurposed into school supplies for underprivileged communities

This approach ensured that waste generated during the event was not only managed responsibly but also redirected toward social good.

Volunteer-Led Execution

The project was driven by a network of 40+ student volunteers who played a critical role in:

  • Enabling on-site waste segregation (plastic, food, paper)
  • Guiding participants toward responsible disposal practices
  • Creating awareness around sustainability and recycling

Their involvement ensured both operational efficiency and meaningful community engagement.

Innovation in Recycling

A key feature of the initiative was the deployment of a Reverse Vending Machine, which processed plastic bottles into recyclable flakes in real time. This demonstrated a scalable and efficient model for event-based plastic waste management, combining technology with on-ground action.

Community Engagement & Inclusion

The initiative extended beyond participants to include sanitation workers and Swachhata Karmis, reinforcing the importance of dignity, collaboration, and shared responsibility in waste management systems.

Large-Scale Public Participation

More than 20,000 participants collectively engaged in a sustainability pledge, committing to:

  • Reduce waste and adopt responsible consumption
  • Protect and conserve natural resources
  • Promote environmentally sustainable practices

Partnerships & Recognition

The initiative was conducted in collaboration with the Snehshilp Foundation, led by Snehal Brahmbatt. The event was attended by Sonu Sood, who acknowledged the importance of sustainability-driven initiatives and community participation.

Project Outcome

The Shilp Marathon initiative demonstrates ARC Foundation's ability to execute large-scale waste management projects, mobilise communities, and integrate sustainability into high-impact public events. It serves as a replicable model for combining environmental responsibility, technology, and citizen engagement to deliver measurable results.
