Overview

Think Off is a debate-led initiative by ARC Foundation that combines intellectual engagement with real-world social impact.

Launched during a critical period, the program created a platform for students to participate in meaningful discussions while contributing to community-focused efforts, including awareness campaigns and fundraising initiatives.

Program Approach

Think Off was designed to move beyond conventional debate formats by linking discussion with action. Participants engaged in:

  • Structured debates on socially relevant topics
  • Dialogue-driven awareness building
  • Contribution to community-oriented initiatives

This approach ensured that intellectual engagement translated into tangible social outcomes.

Social Impact Integration

The initiative successfully bridged the gap between youth discourse and community impact by encouraging participants to actively support causes while engaging in debate. Key focus areas included:

  • Awareness generation on pressing social issues
  • Fundraising and support initiatives during critical times
  • Encouraging responsible and informed civic participation

Participant Engagement

Think Off enabled students to stay engaged, informed, and active during a period where traditional platforms were limited, ensuring continuity in learning, expression, and contribution.

Impact Highlights

  • Integration of debate with social impact initiatives
  • Active student engagement during critical periods
  • Increased awareness and participation in community issues

Project Outcome

Think Off demonstrates how structured debate platforms can be leveraged to drive both intellectual development and social responsibility — creating a model where youth engagement directly contributes to community impact.
